TURKEYWATER FEATURE CONCEPTSPATIAL EXPERIENCE

Two moods of water: calm interior fountains supporting the retail environment and an outdoor musical fountain creating destination-scale spectacle.

INDOOR + OUTDOOR WATER FEATURE VISUALS — TO BE ADDED

DESIGN STRATEGY

Interior features were conceived as restorative pauses within the retail journey, using low, consistent water movement, neutral illumination and dark stone surfaces. Outside, the water experience shifts deliberately toward programmable performance and public gathering.

PROJECT ROLE

Concept design covered fountain identities, water-effect selection, relative scale, day-to-night character and the relationship of each feature to mall circulation and public gathering areas.
