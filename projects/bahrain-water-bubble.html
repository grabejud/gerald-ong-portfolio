BAHRAIN · PEARL-CULTURE AMPHITHEATRE CONCEPT

Bahrain
Water Bubble

CULTURAL DESTINATIONWATERFRONT EXPERIENCEARCHITECTURAL VISUALIZATION

An open shell on the water. Inspired by Bahrain’s pearl-diving heritage, the concept brings gathering, performance, education and spectacle together in a continuous visitor journey.

CULTURAL IDEA

The opened pearl shell becomes a civic-scale landmark with a sheltered performance setting above and an immersive pearl-culture experience below. Terraced seating meets the moving waterfront edge, where water animates the public realm.

ARCHITECTURE & PROGRAMME

A layered amphitheatre, connected by water.

SPATIAL STRATEGY

A distinctive shell canopy frames the performance space; public seating opens toward the water; lower aquarium and exhibition spaces bring visitors closer to the pearl-diving story; and wave effects animate the steps along the waterfront.

VISITOR EXPERIENCE · LIVING HERITAGE

The pearl story, brought to life.

A CONTINUOUS CULTURAL JOURNEY

Live pearl divers create a human-scale encounter. Water walls guide visitors through the interior, while curated pearl displays and archival imagery connect the experience to Bahrain’s maritime memory.
